HOW QUICK DRAFT PICKS A TEAM

Each example below is a REAL simulation against your current Roster — 🔗 marks the main pick, 🛡 a required support, 🛡+ a leftover bonus support. Not hardcoded — reopen this after changing your Roster and it re-runs.

Best available hero of each element+class as the 1st pick, then Autofill for slots 2-5. Greyed out where your Roster has no hero of that combo.

Same fixed 1st pick every time — — then Autofill for the rest, matching real PvP draft order: going 1st means the enemy's Ban Protect isn't known until your Slot 4, so only Slots 4-5 react to it; going 2nd means it's known by your Slot 3, so Slots 3-5 react. Earlier slots are filled exactly as if no Ban Protect element were set at all.
